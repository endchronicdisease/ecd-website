Health happens every day

Chronic disease is the leading cause of illness, death, and disability in America. Many chronic diseases can be prevented or delayed.

End Chronic Disease works to bring prevention into everyday life through education and advocacy while expanding access for those at highest risk.

The bigger picture

Our daily lives shape our health

While genetics play a role in our health, they don’t tell the whole story. What we eat, how we move and sleep, our relationships and experiences, the places where we live, learn, work, and play, the environments we’re exposed to, and our access to quality care can all influence our health over time.

Prevention belongs in each of these places.

Understanding chronic disease

Chronic disease takes many forms, from heart disease and diabetes to autoimmune disease, cancer, neurodegenerative disease, chronic respiratory disease, and many others. It affects people of every age and background, and while genetics can play a role, many other factors can contribute to its development over time.

Bringing prevention into the classroom

Our affiliated 501(c)(4) organization sponsored bipartisan legislation in California to bring evidence-based preventive health education into the state’s K–12 Health Education Framework, including nutrition, physical activity, sleep, stress management, screen use, environmental exposures, and other factors that can influence long-term health.

Good Day Alabama What happens when schools put phones away? Our CEO joined Good Day Alabama to look at what schools are seeing one year after Alabama restricted cell phone use in K–12 public schools, including reports of stronger classroom engagement, improved school culture, less bullying and cheating, and more face-to-face interaction among students. The Wall Street Journal “Never Doubt the Importance of a Jungle Gym” In an opinion letter published by The Wall Street Journal, our CEO makes the case for protecting recess and unstructured play as an important part of children’s health and development.
