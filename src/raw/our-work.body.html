We make prevention understandable

We bring credible science and research into everyday life through media, storytelling, educational content, expert voices, and preventive health education, helping people better understand the factors that shape health and the opportunities to prevent chronic disease and improve health over time.

In the states

Bringing healthy living beyond the classroom

Utah HCR 11, sponsored by our affiliated 501(c)(4) organization, encourages Utah public schools to prioritize daily physical activity and healthy eating education, including annual field trips focused on nutrition and agriculture, while preserving flexibility for local implementation.
